# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the operator of Vibe Trading, monitoring paper workers, market-data freshness, ledger integrity, positions, and strategy performance from the standard dashboard on desktop. The interface must make operational truth visible without requiring database or container access.

## Product Purpose

Vibe Trading provides one governed surface for research, agent workflows, and paper-trading operations. The Paper Trading page must answer which workers are alive, which exchange supplied each cycle, whether that data is fresh, and whether accounting remains reconciled.

## Brand Personality

Precise, governed, calm. The product communicates confidence through accurate state, restrained language, and consistent familiar controls.

## Anti-references

Do not redesign the shipped Vibe Trading dashboard into a separate product. Avoid casino-like crypto styling, decorative neon, invented operational states, misleading green status, and visual effects that obscure data.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the standard Vibe Trading navigation, themes, typography, and component vocabulary.
- Freshness and provenance outrank process reachability.
- Never infer healthy state when telemetry is missing or invalid.
- Keep summary information glanceable and make detailed evidence available in context.
- Use consistent controls and semantic status colors across every operational screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard access. Preserve light and dark themes, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and status labels that do not rely on color alone.
